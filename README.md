# Office Placard — The Guardian God

A one-line museum placard for an ordinary office fire extinguisher, written as if it were an ancient artifact dug from a lost civilisation. Entry for the make.fun bounty "Office Artifacts: The One-Line Placard Contest".

## The entry

> Fire extinguisher, c. 1994. Red steel, untouched. A guardian god, inspected yearly, invoked never. Donated by Health & Safety.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the placard to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The line was chosen by an internal pairwise tournament over 16 candidate placards, judged against the published judge brief (inventiveness of the conceit, then dryness of wit, economy, and label craft) — see `CANDIDATES.md` for the full bracket.
